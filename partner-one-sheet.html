Why explore it?

A potential creator pipeline, not just another catalog

Memory Studio begins before the filmmaker has a finished project. The software is designed to make meaningful scene selection, personal narration and documentary structure more accessible.

A partner could help define the right private-viewing experience, qualification process or professional delivery pathway.

Proposed first steps

Start small; evaluate before committing

  • Meet the appropriate content, product or distribution team
  • Agree on fit, content standards, privacy and technical requirements
  • Define a limited, rights-cleared proof of concept
  • Review results and negotiate any commercial terms separately
Where we stand

Current: Windows v45 development beta with local media import, AI-assisted scene review, chapters, narration, video export and private TV-compatible MP4/USB/HDMI workflows. Not yet available: licensed film catalog, direct OTT publication, native connected-TV app, signed retail installer or existing distribution agreement. Private viewing is distinct from public publishing; public submission requires affirmative consent, documented rights and platform approval.
